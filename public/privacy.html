Privacy

Your doodles and settings

Your drawing and your settings (sound, beat style, tempo, key and scale) are saved in your browser's local storage, so they are still there the next time you open the page. They never leave your device and we cannot see them. To remove them, press Clear in the app or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Technical data when the page loads

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). To deliver the page and keep it secure, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details such as the time of the request and your browser type. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Cloudflare acts as our data processor and may process this data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards set out in its Data Processing Addendum. We keep no logs of our own.

If you contact us

The start page has plain links for writing to us by email or WhatsApp. If you do, we use what you send (your name, your contact details and the content of your message) only to reply and, if you ask for one, to prepare an offer. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)); for any other message it is our legitimate interest in answering it (Article 6(1)(f)).

WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and its own terms and privacy policy apply to what you send there. Because these are plain links, nothing is loaded from Meta until you click one. We keep messages only as long as the conversation needs and delete them if you ask.

What we do not do

  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising.
  • No accounts, sign-ups or forms.
  • No microphone or camera access. All sound is generated on your device.
  • No third-party fonts, scripts, chat widgets or embeds. Everything loads from this site.
