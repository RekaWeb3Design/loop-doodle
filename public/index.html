You are not composing. You are doodling, and the pad does the music part.

  3. 3

    Hit play

    It loops forever. Keep drawing, erasing and swapping sounds while it plays.

What every button does.

You can ignore all of it and just draw. But if you are curious, here is the whole control panel.

The pad

Left to right is time: one trip across is two bars, then it loops. Up and down is pitch, so higher on the pad means a higher note. The faint blocks behind your line show the notes it turned into.

Sound

Five colours, five instruments: Keys, Bass, Lead, Pad and Bells. Pick one, then draw. Put a few on the same pad and you have a band.

Tools

Draw adds lines. Erase removes any line you touch. Undo takes back the last thing, Clear wipes the pad, and Shuffle draws a random doodle for you.

Beat style

The drums under your doodle: Lo-fi, Boom bap, House, Techno, Trap, DnB or Dembow. Same drawing, completely different mood. Each style brings its usual tempo. No beat leaves just your lines.

Tempo

How fast the loop runs, in beats per minute. 70 to 90 is sleepy, 120 to 135 is dancey, 170 is chaos.

Beat level

How loud the drums are next to your doodle. Slide it down to hear the melody, up to make it thump.

Key and scale

The set of notes your lines snap to, which is why nothing sounds wrong. Scale sets the mood: minor is moody, major is sunny, Blues and Japanese have their own flavour. Key shifts everything higher or lower.

Clean space

Wipes the pad and goes full screen with only the colours and a few buttons. Undo brings the old drawing back. Best on a phone.

Open source

Questions people ask.

Short answers, straight from the person who built it.

What is Loop Doodle?

Loop Doodle is a free, open source browser toy that turns your drawings into looping music. You draw a line on the pad, it becomes a melody, and a beat plays underneath. I made it for fun.

Is it free?

Yes. It costs nothing, there are no ads and you never have to sign up.

Do I need to know anything about music?

No. Every line you draw snaps to a scale, so the notes always fit together. Left to right is time, and higher up on the pad is a higher note. That is all there is to know.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, and on a touch screen you draw with your finger. It needs JavaScript and a modern browser. If you hear nothing, check that your phone is not on silent.

Is my drawing uploaded anywhere?

No. The sound is made live in your browser, and your drawing and settings are saved only in your browser's local storage, so they never leave your device. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics.

Can I use the code?

Yes. The code is open source under the MIT licence, so you can read it, fork it and build your own version, as long as you keep the copyright and licence notice. You can find the Loop Doodle code on GitHub. The Loop Doodle name and logo are not covered by the licence.

Who made it?

I did. I am Réka Víg, the founder of The WOW Studio, and I built Loop Doodle for fun.
